# Third-party sources

The brain model is derived from the public FlyWire FAFB v783 connectivity distributed in [Philip Shiu and Nico Spiller's Drosophila brain model repository](https://github.com/philshiu/Drosophila_brain_model). Its MIT notice is preserved in [brain-model-LICENSE.txt](dist/vendor/brain-model-LICENSE.txt). The related paper is [Shiu et al., Nature (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07763-9).

Cell classifications and annotation coordinates come from the public [FlyWire Codex](https://codex.flywire.ai/faq) FAFB v783 snapshot. Original download URLs and checksums are retained in [sources.json](dist/model/sources.json). Please retain the original data attribution and follow the source datasets' usage terms. The graph's root identifiers are omitted from the browser representation.

The bundled Three.js modules retain their MIT license in [three-LICENSE.txt](dist/vendor/three-LICENSE.txt).

The BGM is generated by this project's [composition script](tools/compose-table-lounge.py) without external audio samples. The fly image is an AI-generated visual asset; the interactive 3D fly is constructed by application code.

These notices describe the respective third-party materials and do not change their licenses.
